About the Founder

Chinmaya Patro

Student of Geography, Delhi University · Public policy enthusiast.

Chinmaya Patro is an undergraduate student at the University of Delhi, pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Geography. His academic grounding in spatial systems, urban morphology, and human-environment interactions informs a deep interest in public policy — particularly the intersection of territorial planning, welfare delivery, and environmental governance.

Patro's Policy Parley is his attempt to translate classroom curiosity into accessible, evidence-driven commentary. The platform publishes short analytical essays, policy briefs, and reviews on issues spanning urban reform, climate adaptation, federal fiscal architecture, and digital governance — with an emphasis on clarity over jargon and inquiry over ideology.
